Grilled Chicken Breast

⏱️ 25 mins total Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 large chicken breasts (about 400g total)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • ½ tsp salt, black pepper
  • Juice of half a lemon
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

≈ 54g protein per serving

Method

  1. Mix olive oil, garlic, paprika, oregano, salt, pepper, and lemon juice in a small bowl.
  2. Coat chicken breasts with the marinade. Let sit for 15–20 minutes (or up to 2 hours in the fridge).
  3. Heat a grill pan over medium-high heat. Place chicken and cook for 6–7 minutes per side.
  4. Internal temperature should reach 165°F (74°C). Let the meat rest for 5 minutes.
  5. Slice and garnish with fresh parsley. Serve with roasted veggies or a simple salad.
