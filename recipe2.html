Wild Salmon Power Bowl

⏱️ 30 mins Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets (150g each)
  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 handful fresh spinach
  • ½ cucumber, diced
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Sesame seeds & green onions

≈ 42g protein · omega-3 rich

Method

  1. Pat salmon dry and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Whisk soy sauce, sesame oil, and honey in a small bowl.
  3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat. Sear salmon skin-side down for 4 minutes.
  4. Flip, brush with half the glaze, and cook another 3–4 minutes.
  5. Assemble bowls: quinoa, spinach, cucumber, avocado, and salmon. drizzle remaining glaze, top with sesame seeds and green onions.
