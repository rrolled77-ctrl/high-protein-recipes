Mediterranean Tuna Salad

⏱️ 10 mins · no cook Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 cans albacore tuna (150g each), drained
  • 1 can chickpeas, rinsed
  • ½ red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ cup fresh parsley, chopped
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt, pepper, dried oregano

≈ 32g protein · high fiber

Method

  1. Flake the tuna into a large bowl using a fork.
  2. Add chickpeas, red onion, cherry tomatoes, and parsley.
  3. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon juice. Season with salt, pepper, and oregano.
  4. Toss everything gently until well combined.
  5. Serve immediately or chill for 20 minutes for a refreshing meal. Pairs well with pita or lettuce wraps.
