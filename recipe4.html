Spinach & Feta Omelette

⏱️ 10 mins Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp milk (optional)
  • 1 cup fresh spinach, roughly chopped
  • 30g feta cheese, crumbled
  • 1 tsp butter or olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • Fresh chives for garnish

≈ 26g protein · quick breakfast

Method

  1. Whisk eggs with milk, salt, and pepper until slightly frothy.
  2. Heat butter in a non-stick skillet over medium heat. Add spinach and cook for 30 seconds until wilted.
  3. Pour eggs over spinach. Let set for 30 seconds, then gently push edges toward the center.
  4. Sprinkle feta cheese over half of the omelette. Cook until eggs are almost set.
  5. Fold the omelette in half, slide onto a plate, and garnish with chives.
