Herb-Baked Turkey Meatballs

⏱️ 35 mins Medium

Ingredients

  • 500g lean ground turkey
  • ½ cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • ½ tsp salt, black pepper
  • Olive oil spray

≈ 31g protein per serving

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. In a bowl, combine ground turkey, breadcrumbs, egg, Parmesan, garlic, oregano, basil, salt, and pepper. Mix gently with your hands.
  3. Roll mixture into 20–24 meatballs (about 1.5 inches each).
  4. Place on the baking sheet, lightly spray with olive oil. Bake for 18–20 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  5. Serve with marinara sauce, over pasta, or alongside roasted vegetables.
